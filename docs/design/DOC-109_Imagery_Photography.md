# DOC-109 — Imagery & Photography

**Document ID:** DOC-109  
**Title:** Imagery & Photography  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Product & Design

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the visual standards for all photography, imagery, and media used throughout the ARCHIVE platform.

Photography is the primary visual language of the product.

The interface exists to showcase products—not to compete with them.

---

# 2. Philosophy

Users come to ARCHIVE to discover fashion.

Photography is therefore the single most important visual asset.

Excellent photography builds trust faster than any interface element.

Poor photography cannot be compensated for by good UI.

---

# 3. Visual Priority

Every screen should prioritize content in the following order:

1. Product Photography
2. Product Information
3. Primary Actions
4. Seller Information
5. Secondary Content
6. Interface Chrome

The interface must always remain visually subordinate.

---

# 4. Photography Style

Preferred imagery should feel:

- Natural
- Premium
- Editorial
- Authentic
- Timeless
- High resolution
- Well composed

Photography should resemble premium fashion editorials rather than commercial product catalogs.

---

# 5. Lighting

Preferred lighting:

- Natural daylight
- Soft studio lighting
- Even exposure
- Neutral white balance

Avoid:

- Harsh flash
- Heavy shadows
- Colored lighting
- Dramatic effects
- Artificial filters

---

# 6. Backgrounds

Preferred backgrounds:

- White
- Off-white
- Neutral gray
- Soft concrete
- Stone
- Minimal interior
- Clean architectural spaces

Avoid distracting environments.

Backgrounds must support the product.

---

# 7. Product Visibility

Products must occupy most of the frame.

Avoid excessive empty space around products.

Important product details should remain clearly visible.

Do not crop essential parts of an item.

---

# 8. Image Quality

Minimum quality requirements:

- Sharp focus
- Correct exposure
- Accurate colors
- Low compression
- No visible artifacts

Images that fail these standards should be flagged during listing creation.

---

# 9. Color Accuracy

Photography should represent products honestly.

Avoid excessive editing.

Avoid oversaturation.

Avoid unrealistic contrast.

Trust is more important than visual drama.

---

# 10. Product Angles

Recommended minimum:

- Front
- Back
- Detail
- Label
- Material close-up

Additional images are encouraged when they improve buyer confidence.

---

# 11. Image Count

Recommended:

5–10 photographs per listing.

Minimum:

3 photographs.

Maximum:

20 photographs.

Quality is always preferred over quantity.

---

# 12. Aspect Ratio

Primary product ratio:

4:5

Alternative ratios:

1:1

3:4

Maintain consistent presentation across listings.

Avoid inconsistent gallery layouts.

---

# 13. Gallery Behaviour

The first image is the most important.

Gallery navigation should feel immediate.

Transitions should remain subtle.

Users should never lose context while browsing images.

---

# 14. Image Cropping

Cropping should preserve:

- Product proportions
- Symmetry
- Important details

Never crop solely to fill available space.

---

# 15. Zoom

Every product image should support zoom.

Zoom must preserve image quality.

Interaction should feel immediate.

Avoid loading delays whenever possible.

---

# 16. Video

Videos are optional.

Recommended duration:

10–30 seconds.

Purpose:

- Show materials
- Show movement
- Demonstrate condition

Avoid promotional editing.

Avoid background music.

---

# 17. Editorial Content

Editorial imagery may be used for:

- Collections
- Landing pages
- Campaigns
- Brand stories

Editorial imagery should never distract from marketplace functionality.

---

# 18. Icons

Icons support navigation.

Icons never replace clear labels.

Preferred characteristics:

- Minimal
- Consistent stroke
- Simple geometry
- Neutral appearance

Avoid decorative icon sets.

---

# 19. Illustrations

Illustrations should be extremely rare.

Allowed primarily for:

- Empty states
- Onboarding
- Educational content

Illustrations should remain monochrome or neutral.

Avoid playful artwork.

---

# 20. Avatars

Seller avatars should appear circular.

Default avatar should be minimal.

Avoid colorful placeholder graphics.

Verified sellers should be identified using a badge rather than a unique avatar style.

---

# 21. Brand Logos

Brand logos must retain their original proportions.

Never recolor logos.

Never stretch or distort them.

Provide sufficient surrounding whitespace.

---

# 22. Placeholder Images

When no product image exists:

Use a neutral placeholder.

Avoid decorative graphics.

Avoid stock photography.

The placeholder should encourage users to upload photos.

---

# 23. Image Loading

Images should load progressively.

Skeleton placeholders should match the final layout.

Avoid sudden layout shifts.

Preserve aspect ratio before loading completes.

---

# 24. Accessibility

All meaningful images must include descriptive alternative text.

Decorative images should be ignored by assistive technologies.

Image controls must remain keyboard accessible.

---

# 25. AI Generated Images

AI-generated imagery may only be used for:

- Marketing concepts
- Educational content
- Editorial illustrations

AI-generated product photographs are prohibited unless clearly disclosed.

Authenticity is fundamental to user trust.

---

# 26. Content Moderation

Images should be automatically reviewed for:

- Nudity
- Violence
- Hate symbols
- Counterfeit indicators
- Watermarks
- Copyright violations
- Low image quality

Potential violations should be reviewed before publication.

---

# 27. AI Implementation Rules

AI tools must preserve photography as the dominant visual element.

Layouts must never reduce image prominence to accommodate additional interface components.

Images should always remain the primary focal point.

---

# 28. Definition of Success

Photography should communicate quality, authenticity, and trust before users read a single word.

The interface should behave like the walls of a premium gallery—present, supportive, and intentionally unobtrusive.
